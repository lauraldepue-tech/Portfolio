About Laura

An artist's imagination. A designer's perspective.

Hello! I'm Laura, a multidisciplinary designer, artist, and creative technologist.

My work lives at the intersection of visual storytelling, technology, and human-centered design.

I enjoy taking complicated ideas, imagining new possibilities, and transforming them into visual and interactive experiences that people can explore, understand, and enjoy.

My professional background includes more than a decade of experience in higher education, digital experience design, multimedia production, accessibility, and creative project development.

I've collaborated with multidisciplinary teams, subject matter experts, designers, and technology professionals to develop experiences for diverse audiences.

Today, I'm bringing those skills together with my passion for illustration, storytelling, interactive experiences, and emerging creative technology.

What I bring to a project

A combination of creative thinking, visual communication, technical curiosity, research, accessibility, and experience managing complex projects from concept to completion.
