Surviving the Border

An interactive, choice-driven story developed with Begum Sacak using the ARIS platform. Players follow a fictional Honduran teenager navigating the risks of migration, with decisions shaping the experience and its possible outcomes.

The project explored how interactive narrative can influence empathy, knowledge, and emotional response. In the study, 72% of participants reported increased empathy toward Latin American immigrants.

My role: Co-designer and research collaborator · Focus: narrative, interaction, research

How I work

About me

A researcher’s questions.
A storyteller’s instincts.

I’m a multidisciplinary designer with a background spanning higher education, student success, learning experience design, and digital learning. I’m especially drawn to work that combines clear systems with narrative, meaningful choices, and visual communication.

My approach is both analytical and creative: understand the people and context, find the through-line, and build an experience that respects the user’s attention and agency. I care about accessibility not as a final checklist, but as part of the design from the beginning.
