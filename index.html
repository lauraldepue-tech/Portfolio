Gamification & LMS Choice Architecture

Pokémon: Global and Local Cultures

Redesigned a full-semester anthropology course into a 6-week, fully accessible online learning path. Pushed the structural limits of the LMS by building an intricate multi-track linking system where students choose 1 of 3 character paths (Wizard, Gamer, or Kenshusei) to navigate the curriculum.

Replaced high-stakes exams with Pecha Kucha presentations and book reviews, integrating badge rewards and extra-credit "Genius Certificate" modules.

Interactive Narrative & Scenario-Based Learning

Surviving the Border (Mobile Game & LX Research)

Designed a branching mobile interactive scenario on the ARIS platform where players experience the journey of a 17-year-old Honduran youth navigating sociopolitical choices to cross the U.S. border.

Conducted formal empirical research evaluating empathy evoke mechanics and sociocultural awareness. Post-game findings revealed that 72% of participants experienced measurably increased empathy through narrative decision architecture.
